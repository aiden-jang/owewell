# Visitor walkthrough

Use this with someone who has not used Owewell. Ask them to think aloud without
explaining the controls first. Record where they hesitate, what they expect and
whether the result matches their own arithmetic. This is a prepared review, not
evidence that a visitor study has taken place.

## Public calculator

1. Open the public splitter in a fresh browser. Ask what the app does and whether
   an account is needed. Can they find and try the labeled example?
2. Split a bill between three people, choose who paid and add a tip. Ask them to
   explain their own share and who owes whom.
3. Copy the answer, reload and recover the editable draft. Check that they
   understand saving to an account is different from copying the answer.

## Groups and shared tabs

Use a disposable local account and group. Never ask for another person's password
or put private receipts in screenshots.

1. Create a group, add a name-only member and record an expense.
2. Open its shared link in a signed-out browser. Ask whether viewing joins the
   group and what “This is me” would connect to an account.
3. Record a partial payment. Ask whether money was actually transferred. The app
   records payments; it does not send them.
4. Disconnect, queue an expense and reconnect. Ask which amounts are saved and
   which are waiting. Confirm the queued item is recorded once.
5. Open another group and return. Check the group name, balance and actions all
   belong to the group currently shown.

## Device checks

On an actual phone, try the number keyboard, long names, bill details, sticky
sheet actions and browser Back. Test an installed app losing its connection and
receiving an update with unsaved work. Use VoiceOver or TalkBack to hear labels,
errors, dialogs and payment confirmations. Desktop emulation and engine tests
cannot establish those physical-device results.

Log the date, device/browser, completed task, hesitation or failure and its impact.
Keep observations separate from proposed solutions; prioritize mistakes affecting
money or membership before cosmetic preferences.
